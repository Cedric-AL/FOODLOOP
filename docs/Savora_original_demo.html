From surplus to support

One visible handoff, from listing to delivery.

1

Submit the details

Food category, quantity, packaging condition, storage, and the available pickup window are recorded in one place.

2

Basic screening

Savora checks the submitted condition, storage, and expiration or best-before information for completeness.

3

Find a suitable match

Nearby organizations review the details and request food that fits their needs, capacity, and location.

4

Coordinate the handoff

Donors, recipients, and authorized volunteers agree on pickup or delivery while Savora keeps the record.

Screening is not certification. Savora documents user-provided information and applies basic checks. It does not inspect food, guarantee safety, or replace professional assessment.

Shared with care

Less waste. More care.

Built around the people making the handoff happen.

The platform organizes the record; people move the food. Donors, recipients, and authorized volunteers coordinate the practical details together, with a clear trail of what was offered and what happened next.

  • Basic screening and documentation
  • Needs- and location-aware matching
  • Timestamped handoff records
